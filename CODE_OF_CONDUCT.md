# Puzzary community code of conduct

## Our standard

Puzzary contributors and participants are expected to be respectful,
constructive and welcoming. Disagreement about code or product direction is
normal; harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption and publication of another person's private information are not.

Give actionable feedback, assume good faith, respect boundaries and focus on
the work. Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that do not
meet this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

## Reporting conduct concerns

For ordinary moderation, contact the maintainer through the
[`barrelcollector` GitHub profile](https://github.com/barrelcollector). Do not
publish sensitive personal details in an issue. Security vulnerabilities belong
in the private process described by `SECURITY.md`.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Enforcement
will consider context, impact, prior behavior and the safety of affected people.
Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is prohibited.
