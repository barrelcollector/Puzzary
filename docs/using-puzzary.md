# Using Puzzary

Puzzary's web interface is the primary way to browse, play, print, and share
puzzles. The available actions depend on your account permissions and the
modules installed by the administrator.

## Browse and play

Open **Library** to search and filter the puzzles available to your account.
Each puzzle page provides the play surface, instructions, hints, progress,
printing, and collaboration controls supported by that puzzle's module.

Progress belongs to your account. Library cards distinguish puzzles that are
not started, in progress, or complete. Resetting a puzzle removes your saved
progress and attempts for that puzzle.

## Group Solve

Open **Group Solve** from the navigation or a supported puzzle page to join by
code, browse public sessions, or host a new session when your role allows it.
The host controls membership, guests, privacy, and session settings. See the
[Group Solve guide](group-solve.md) for details.

## Print and submit work

Use **Print** on a supported puzzle to open its spoiler-free print layout and
download the formats offered by the server. A printed sheet may include a link
or QR code back to the online puzzle.

Where enabled, **Upload Work** accepts a photo of completed paper work. Your
uploads remain private to you and authorized reviewers; they are not public
static files.

## Offline play and installation

Eligible puzzles can be saved from their puzzle page and managed under
**Offline**. Offline availability is set per puzzle and also depends on its
module. Protected or otherwise sensitive puzzles may be intentionally
online-only. See [offline puzzle play](offline-puzzle-play.md).

Puzzary can also be installed as a Progressive Web App on supported devices.
See the [PWA guide](progressive-web-app.md) for browser and HTTPS requirements.

## Feeds and notifications

From **Account**, create private RSS or Atom feed URLs and opt in to browser
notifications. Both support filters such as puzzle type, theme, tag, or
creator. Feed URLs are credentials: do not publish them. Browser notifications
are configured separately on each device.

- [Private RSS and Atom feeds](rss-atom-feeds.md)
- [Browser notifications](browser-notifications.md)

## Account and appearance

The **Account** page shows personal activity and statistics and provides the
settings your role can change. Choose Light, Dark, Follow System, or an
administrator-provided theme. If the installation uses optional OpenID Connect,
the sign-in page may also offer an external provider; local accounts remain the
default unless the administrator changes the login mode.
